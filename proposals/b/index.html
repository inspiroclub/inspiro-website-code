Why it works

We don't ask anyone to give up their phone. We make the evening worth putting it down for. What research says, including where it is weaker than headlines suggest:

20–40 min

less phone use a day in greyscale

Making the phone duller cut daily use by about 39 minutes in one US student study and about 20 minutes in a pre-registered one-week Dutch study.

Holte and Ferraro, 2020; Dekker and Baumgartner, 2023.

Full

energy back after a nature break

With 308 employees, a nature break fully restored energy; a social media break only partly; no break was worst.

Grobelny and colleagues, Scientific Reports, 2024.

57%

of students would prefer no TikTok at all

And 58% would prefer no Instagram. Stepping away alone feels costly; stepping away together doesn't.

Bursztyn and colleagues, American Economic Review, 2025.

Mixed

results for digital detox

Reviews find mixed effects and cravings during abstinence. That's why we offer better alternatives rather than detox challenges.

Radtke and colleagues, systematic review, 2022.

Several of these studies appear in the further reading for Steven Bartlett's October 2026 episode with Dr. Sahar Yousef. Each will be checked against the original paper before this page goes live.

How Inspiro started

Three countries, three children, and one lesson: rules alone don't win against a screen. A better offline life does.

Two weeks without limits

Between 2018 and 2022 we lived with our three children in Spain, Brazil and Romania. In 2020, in Brazil, we shared a home with a family that set no screen limits. Within two weeks our children were struggling. Later, the smartphone arguments at home taught us the same thing again.

Rules helped, but they didn't change much. What changed things was an evening good enough to put the phone down for. In our words: sustainable change happens through environments, not willpower.

What we are building

An ethical, sustainable community, starting in Timișoara and growing across Romania and Europe. No manipulation, no consumerism. Children, parents, young adults and grown-ups meet in person, and young adults become contributors and leaders.

Our aim for 2030: Romanian families leading in digital wellbeing, through better real-life experiences rather than restriction.
